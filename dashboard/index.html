Rainfall Scenario Sensitivity & Screening Assessment

Screening-level flood depths were evaluated across three 24h rainfall inputs: Scenario A (Urban-mean 153.1 mm), Scenario B (Domain-max 245.0 mm), and Scenario C (Spatially varying 2D IMERG field 129.9–176.1 mm).

Qualitative Finding: Higher rainfall scenarios produce progressively deeper inundation across low-lying coastal depressions (Velachery, Pallikaranai, Mudichur). Due to low relief in the coastal plain ($0–15\text{ m}$), uniform precipitation inputs produce step-wise depth tier transitions governed by discrete land-cover runoff coefficients. The screening model is presented qualitatively as a rapid hazard screening indicator rather than definitive hydrodynamic extents.
